## Attendance
Students must attend at least 80% of the practical sessions (werkcolleges) of every module. Lectures are not mandatory but strongly recommended. If your attendance drops below 80%, you must complete a catch-up assignment, agreed with your module coordinator, before you are allowed to take the exam.

## Reporting sick
If you are too ill to attend an exam or to meet a deadline, report sick via the Student Portal before 09:00 on the day itself. Reports made after 09:00 are not accepted, unless you can show a doctor's note. After a sick report for an exam, you must have a conversation with your study advisor within 5 working days.

## Late submissions
Assignments that are handed in late lose 10% of the maximum grade for every started period of 24 hours. After 72 hours, no submission is accepted anymore and the grade for that assignment is 1.0.

## Resits
Every exam has two attempts per academic year: the regular exam and one resit. The resit takes place in the second week of the next block. A resit grade is capped at 7.0, unless you had a valid sick report for the regular exam. In that case the cap does not apply.

## Passing grades
A grade of 5.5 or higher is a pass. Every module is worth 5 ECTS. Compensation between components of the same module is not possible: each component must be at least 5.5 to pass the module.

## Binding study advice
At the end of the first year you must have earned at least 48 of the 60 ECTS to continue the programme. Students who score lower receive a negative binding study advice and have to leave the programme.

## Internship
The internship takes place in year 3 and lasts 20 weeks, full-time. You can only start after you have earned at least 120 ECTS. The internship must be approved by the internship coordinator before you sign a contract. The internship agreement has to be signed by the student, the company and the school.

## Graduation project
The graduation project takes place in year 4 and is worth 30 ECTS. It is supervised by one lecturer and one supervisor from the company. Every graduation project must pass an ethics check by the AI Ethics Review Board before the project starts.

## AI Ethics Review Board
Every project that uses data about people must be submitted to the AI Ethics Review Board at least 3 weeks before the data collection starts. The board meets every second Thursday of the month. Projects without approval may not collect or process personal data.

## Using generative AI in assignments
Generative AI tools such as chatbots and code assistants may be used for brainstorming and coding help, as long as you add an AI usage statement to every submission. In this statement you describe which tool you used and for what purpose. Generative AI is not allowed during written exams. Using AI without declaring it counts as fraud.

## Fraud and plagiarism
On a first offence the exam or assignment is declared invalid and the case is reported to the Examination Board. On a second offence the Examination Board can exclude you from exams for up to one year.

## AI Lab and hardware
The AI Lab (room B2.14) is open Monday to Friday from 08:30 to 21:00. Students can borrow a laptop with a GPU for up to 2 weeks; reserve it at the lab desk. Our GPU cluster is called Zwaluw. You can log in with your student account. Jobs may run for at most 24 hours and each student may run at most 2 jobs at the same time.

## Software
Students can use PyCharm Professional for free through the school licence. The standard Python version in all courses is Python 3.12. Jupyter notebooks are used in the data and AI modules.

## Printing
Every student receives 150 free print credits per academic year. Extra credits can be bought at the service desk for 5 cents per page.

## Study advisor and wellbeing
Your study advisor is Fatima Okafor, in room A1.07. Walk-in hours are on Tuesday and Thursday from 13:00 to 15:00. The student psychologist offers up to 5 free sessions per student per academic year.

## Extra exam time
Students with dyslexia or another documented condition can get 25% extra time during exams. Request this via your study advisor at least 4 weeks before the start of the exam period.

## Exchange semester
An exchange semester abroad is possible in the first or second semester of year 3. Partner universities are located in Finland, Spain and Canada. Applications must be submitted before 1 March of the previous academic year.
